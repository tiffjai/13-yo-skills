# Brainstorming examples

Treat these as response patterns, not answers to copy. Adapt to actual constraints.

## A repetitive office task

**Prompt:** "I copy figures between two spreadsheets every Friday. Brainstorm something useful; I have an hour and no software budget."

**Response pattern:**

- Define the goal as reducing repetitive transfer errors and time, not automatically building an AI agent.
- Separate the necessary reporting outcome from the assumed copy-and-paste method.
- Explore distinct mechanisms: formulas; removing duplicate collection; a standard import template; checking only exceptions; a small local script if allowed.
- Recommend the most feasible approach after checking available tools and permissions. A spreadsheet feature may be better than an LLM.
- Propose a one-hour test using fabricated rows; compare completion time and known errors against a manual baseline.
- State that a clean demo is not proof of production reliability. Do not upload the user's employer's files.

## A genuinely open creative brief

**Prompt:** "Give me unusual ideas for a community book club. No budget."

**Response pattern:**

Offer distinct formats such as a read-one-page gathering, a bookless story exchange, a shared walking discussion, a quiet parallel-reading session and a choose-your-own-ending evening. Explain who each format helps and its main practical constraint. Recommend one small voluntary trial with a simple participation signal. Avoid invented claims about what the community wants.

## Written Cantonese

**Prompt:** "我想整個幫人揀職業方向嘅 app，但唔知由邊開始。用廣東話。"

**Illustrative excerpt:**

「你想幫人由『好多可能』行到『知道下一步試咩』。最值得先驗證嘅假設係：佢哋係缺乏資訊，定係唔知點比較自己重視嘅嘢？

可以試三個唔同方向：做工作內容比較、提供一個半小時嘅模擬任務，或者幫人記錄試做之後嘅感受。以零預算起步，我會先整一頁模擬任務；睇吓人做完會唔會更清楚自己想了解咩。呢個只能測試有冇幫助，未證明佢哋願意付費。」

## Avoid

- "Your genius idea will definitely disrupt the market."
- Five chatbot ideas with different names but the same mechanism.
- Treating unauthorized access or spending as part of a harmless demo.

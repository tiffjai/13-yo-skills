# Tiny-experiment examples

## A finance workflow prototype

**Prompt:** "I want to see if a tool can catch mismatches between two reports. I have 45 minutes and cannot share work files."

**Experiment card:**

- **Question:** Can a simple comparison identify the mismatch types we care about?
- **Action:** Make two synthetic tables with 20 rows, including deliberately missing IDs, duplicates and amount differences. Define the expected findings before running the comparison.
- **Budget:** 45 minutes; no paid services; no confidential files.
- **Observation:** Which seeded cases were found, missed or incorrectly flagged? Compare with the expected list.
- **Decision:** If results match the expected cases, investigate a larger authorized test. If they do not, inspect matching logic and definitions. Do not describe this as demonstrated accuracy on real reports.
- **Stop:** No production access or uploads of work data. Stop when the time budget ends.
- **Review:** At the end of the session, record the main limitation and whether a further test is justified.

## A possible career direction

**Prompt:** "I might like teaching. Can I test that without changing jobs?"

Offer a realistic small activity: prepare and deliver a short explanation to a willing adult friend, or explore an authorized observation opportunity. Ask for permission before involving anyone. Record the parts enjoyed, preparation burden and feedback about clarity. Explain that one friendly audience does not establish day-to-day teaching fit. Do not treat a person as an unsuspecting experimental subject.

## A failed attempt

**Prompt:** "I watched two coding courses and still don't know if software work suits me."

Recognize that course-watching has not answered the work-fit question. Change the method to a small realistic build or debugging task with a firm time limit and suitable support. Look for engagement with the task, not just completion. Consider lack of prerequisite knowledge as an alternative explanation for difficulty.

## Written Cantonese

**Prompt:** "我想試寫作，但每日都寫會太攰。有冇細啲嘅試法？"

**Illustrative excerpt:**

「可以試今個星期只寫一次、十五分鐘，揀你本身想講嘅題目。想知道嘅係：呢種寫法有冇令你想再寫，而唔係你夠唔夠自律。

寫完記一句：邊部分有趣、邊部分攰？如果唔想再寫，可以改題目、改用錄音，或者暫停。一次試作提供線索，未足以判斷你適唔適合做作家。」

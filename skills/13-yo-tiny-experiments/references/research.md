# Research notes: learning from a small test

- **Chen et al. (2025), A strategic mindset.** Two randomized experiments with preschool children examined strategy generation and delay of gratification. Use the distinction between repeating an ineffective method and seeking an alternative as inspiration. Do not describe prototype testing or adult career experiments as outcomes of that study. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001916).
- **Zheng et al., Take a Step Back (ICLR 2024).** Abstraction before reasoning improved performance on several tasks in the tested LLMs. Returning from a stalled method to the underlying question is a related design idea, not a replicated result for this skill. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06117).

Treat the experiment card, zero-spend default and two-uninformative-attempts trigger as authored conventions. They are not validated psychological thresholds. Treat observations as local evidence; informal trials do not establish causality, clinical benefit, market demand or permanent personal traits.
